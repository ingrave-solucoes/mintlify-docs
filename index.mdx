Chatwoot is an open-source customer engagement platform that brings all your customer conversations into a single, collaborative inbox. Whether your customers reach you through your website, WhatsApp, email, Facebook, Instagram, or SMS — your team handles everything from one place.

## How Chatwoot Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect your channels">
    Add a live-chat widget to your website, connect your WhatsApp number, link your email inbox, or integrate Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, and more — all in under five minutes each.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite your team">
    Add agents and organize them into teams. Assign conversations manually or let Chatwoot route them automatically with round-robin assignment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reply from one inbox">
    Every message from every channel lands in a single dashboard. Reply, assign labels, set priorities, and collaborate with private notes — without switching tabs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Measure and improve">
    Track response times, resolution rates, and CSAT scores with built-in reports. Use automation rules to keep your queue clean and your SLAs on track.
  </Step>
</Steps>
