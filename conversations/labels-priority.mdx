Keeping a busy support queue organized requires more than just assigning conversations to agents. Labels let you tag conversations with meaningful categories — like "billing", "bug-report", or "premium-customer" — while priority levels signal how urgently each one needs attention. Together, they give your team a fast, reliable way to sort, filter, and focus on what matters most.

## Creating Labels

Labels are account-wide, so any label you create is available to all agents and can be applied across every inbox and team.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Label Settings">
    Go to **Settings → Labels** in the sidebar, then click the **Add Label** button in the top-right corner.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the label details">
    Complete the form using the fields described below.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the label">
    Click **Create**. A confirmation message — *"Label added successfully"* — confirms the label is ready to use.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| **Label Name** | A short, descriptive name for the category. Only letters, numbers, hyphens (`-`), and underscores (`_`) are allowed. Example: `premium_customer` |
| **Description** | A brief explanation of when to use this label. Example: *"Issues that must be resolved as a top priority."* |
| **Colour** | Pick a colour to make the label visually distinct in the UI. Click the colour swatch, make your selection, then click the swatch again to confirm. |
| **Show Label on Sidebar** | Enable this to pin the label to your left sidebar as a quick-access filter for all conversations carrying that label. |

<Tip>
Use distinct colours for labels that represent urgency or escalation paths — it makes them easy to spot at a glance in the conversation list.
</Tip>

## Applying Labels to a Conversation

Once labels exist in your account, any agent can apply them to a conversation at any time.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the conversation">
    Click on the conversation you want to label.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find Conversation Labels in the sidebar">
    In the right-hand **Conversation Details** panel, locate the **Conversation Labels** section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a label">
    Click **Add Labels** and select one or more labels from the dropdown. You can apply multiple labels to a single conversation.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To remove a label, click the **×** next to the label name in the same panel.

## Editing and Deleting Labels

Go to **Settings → Labels** to manage your existing labels. Each label in the list has an **edit** icon and a **delete** icon beside it.

- Click the **edit** icon to update the name, description, colour, or sidebar visibility.
- Click the **delete** icon to permanently remove the label. Removing a label also removes it from any conversations it was applied to.

## Priority Levels

Chatwoot offers four priority levels to signal how urgently a conversation needs attention. Assign a priority when the volume or complexity of your queue means some issues must jump ahead of others.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Urgent" icon="circle-exclamation">
    Reserve for the most critical issues that must be handled immediately above everything else. Use this sparingly so it retains its meaning.
  </Card>
  <Card title="High" icon="arrow-up">
    Important conversations that need a timely response but aren't blocking a customer completely.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Medium" icon="minus">
    The standard level for most conversations — important but not time-critical.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Low" icon="arrow-down">
    Routine issues or informational requests that can be handled when the queue allows.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Setting a Priority

You can set a conversation's priority in three ways:

**From the sidebar:** Open the conversation and find the **Conversation Priority** dropdown in the right-hand panel. Select the priority level that fits the situation.

**From the context menu:** Right-click any conversation in the conversation list to open its context menu, then choose a priority level directly — without opening the conversation.

**From the command palette:** Press `Cmd + K` (macOS) or `Ctrl + K` (Windows/Linux) to open the command palette. Type `Assign priority`, press **Enter**, choose the desired level, and press **Enter** again.

Once set, the priority badge is visible in the conversation list and a log entry appears in the conversation activity feed to record when the priority was changed and by whom.

## Filtering Conversations by Label or Priority

Use Chatwoot's sidebar and filter tools to quickly narrow the conversation list.

**By label:** If you enabled **Show Label on Sidebar** when creating a label, click that label in the left sidebar to instantly see only conversations tagged with it.

**By priority or label using filters:** In the **Conversations** view, click the **Filter** icon at the top of the conversation list. Add a filter condition for **Priority** (choosing Urgent, High, Medium, or Low) or for **Label** (choosing any label in your account). Apply multiple filter conditions at once to create a precise view.

## Using Folders (Segments) for Saved Filters

If you find yourself applying the same combination of filters repeatedly, save it as a **Segment** (also called a Folder) so it appears as a persistent view in your sidebar.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set up your filters">
    Apply the label, priority, or other filter conditions you want to save in the conversation list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save as Segment">
    Click **Save Segment**, give it a name, and confirm. The segment appears in the left sidebar under **Folders** for one-click access.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use the segment">
    Click the segment name in the sidebar any time to load that filtered view instantly.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
Segments are personal — a segment you create is visible only to you, not to other agents in your account.
</Info>
