Every conversation in Chatwoot begins when a customer reaches out through one of your connected channels — a live-chat widget, email inbox, or social integration. From that first message, the conversation moves through a series of statuses that help your team track what needs attention, what's waiting, and what's been resolved.

## Conversation Statuses

Chatwoot uses four statuses to represent where a conversation stands at any point in its lifecycle. Understanding these statuses is the foundation of working efficiently in the platform.

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Pending** | Used when a bot integration is active. The conversation is held in a temporary state while the bot triages it. Once the bot hands off, the status changes to Open. Agents can also manually move a conversation back to Pending to return it to bot handling. |
| **Open** | The default status for new conversations when no bot is configured. The customer is waiting for a reply from your team. |
| **Snoozed** | The conversation is temporarily set aside. Use this when you need more information, are waiting on a third party, or want to set a reminder to follow up later. The conversation automatically returns to Open based on the snooze option you choose. |
| **Resolved** | The conversation has been addressed and requires no further action. Equivalent to closing a support ticket. |

<Info>
When an agent changes a conversation's status to **Open**, Chatwoot automatically assigns that conversation to the agent making the change.
</Info>

## Snooze Options

When you snooze a conversation, choose the option that matches when you expect to revisit it.

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Snooze Until Next Reply" icon="reply">
    The conversation returns to Open as soon as the customer sends another message.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Snooze Until Tomorrow" icon="sun">
    The conversation returns to Open the next day, giving you overnight to investigate.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Snooze Until Next Week" icon="calendar">
    The conversation returns to Open at the start of next week for longer-term follow-ups.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Replying to a Customer

To send a reply, open the conversation and type your message in the **Reply** area at the bottom of the chat window. Press **Shift + Enter** to add a new line without sending. When you're ready to send, click the **Send** button.

The Reply area includes several tools to enrich your messages:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add an emoji">
    Click the emoji button to open the emoji picker and insert an emoji anywhere in your message.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attach a file">
    Click the attachment button to upload and send a file, image, or document alongside your reply.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch to Rich Text">
    Click the rich text editor button to enable formatting options — bold, italic, bullet lists, links, and more. When you're done formatting, tick the checkbox to send. Click the rich text button again to return to plain text.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Using Canned Responses in a Reply

Canned responses are pre-saved reply templates you can pull into the editor instantly. In the Reply area, type `/` followed by the shortcode of the canned response you want. A list of matching responses appears — select the one you need and it fills the editor automatically.

<Tip>
See [Canned Responses](/conversations/canned-responses) for a full guide on creating and managing your saved reply templates.
</Tip>

## Sending an Email Transcript

Once a customer has provided their email address during the conversation, you can send them a full transcript of the chat.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the conversation menu">
    Click the **three-dot menu (⋯)** in the top-right corner of the conversation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Send Transcript">
    Choose **Send Transcript** from the dropdown.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the recipient">
    Select **Send the transcript to the assigned agent** to forward it to your team, or select **Send the transcript to another email address** and enter the customer's email to send it directly to them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit">
    Click **Submit**. You'll see a confirmation message: *"The chat transcript was sent successfully."*
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Once a conversation is marked **Resolved**, the customer can also request a transcript themselves directly from the chat widget by clicking **Request a conversation transcript**.
</Note>

## Viewing Previous Conversations

When a returning customer starts a new conversation, Chatwoot surfaces their previous conversation history in the sidebar. This gives you immediate context about past issues without needing to search manually.

To view a previous conversation, click the customer's name listed under **Previous Conversations** in the right-hand panel. The full history of that conversation opens for your reference. If the customer has had multiple past conversations, all of them appear in the list so you can review whichever is most relevant.

## Deleting a Message

If you need to remove a message you've already sent, hover over the message to reveal the **three-dot menu (⋯)** beside it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the message menu">
    Click the **⋯** icon next to the message you want to remove.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Delete">
    Choose **Delete** from the options. The message is permanently removed from the conversation thread.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
Deleting a message is permanent and cannot be undone. The customer will no longer be able to see the deleted message.
</Warning>
