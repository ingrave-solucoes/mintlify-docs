Chatwoot gives you full control over who handles each conversation. You can manually route a conversation to a specific agent or team, let the round-robin system distribute incoming messages automatically, or transfer a conversation to a different agent mid-stream. Getting assignments right means faster responses and a more organised support queue.

## Manually Assigning an Agent

Assign a conversation to the agent best suited to handle it directly from the conversation details panel.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the conversation">
    Click on the conversation in your inbox to open it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Locate the Assigned Agent field">
    In the right-hand **Conversation Details** sidebar, find the **Assigned Agent** dropdown.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select an agent">
    Click the dropdown and choose the agent you want to assign. Only agents who have access to the inbox where the conversation was created — and all administrators — appear in this list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
You can reassign a conversation to a different agent at any time by repeating these steps and selecting a new name from the dropdown.
</Note>

## Assigning a Conversation to a Team

Teams let you route conversations to a group of agents who share a responsibility area, such as Billing, Technical Support, or Sales.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the conversation">
    Click on the conversation you want to assign.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Locate the Assigned Team field">
    In the right-hand sidebar under **Conversation Actions**, find the **Assigned Team** dropdown.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a team">
    Choose the appropriate team from the list. All agents in that team receive a notification about the assignment.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
Assigning a team first and then an agent within that team is a good practice — it ensures the conversation appears in the correct team queue even if the specific agent is unavailable.
</Tip>

## Round-Robin Auto-Assignment

Chatwoot includes a built-in round-robin system that distributes incoming conversations equally among online agents. This removes the need to manually assign every conversation and prevents any single agent from being overloaded.

**How it works:**

- When a new conversation arrives, Chatwoot checks which agents with inbox access are currently **online**.
- It then assigns the conversation to the next agent in rotation, distributing the load evenly.
- If **all agents are offline**, the conversation remains **unassigned** until an agent comes online or a manual assignment is made.

### Enabling Round-Robin for an Inbox

Round-robin assignment is enabled by default for new inboxes. To verify or change the setting:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Inbox Settings">
    Navigate to **Settings → Inboxes** and click the **Settings** link for the inbox you want to configure.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scroll to the Round-Robin section">
    Scroll down until you see the **Round-Robin** assignment toggle.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable the setting">
    Set the toggle to **Enabled** and click **Update** to save your changes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
Round-robin assignment only considers agents who are marked as **online** at the time the conversation comes in. Agents set to **Busy** or **Offline** are skipped in the rotation.
</Info>

## Transferring a Conversation to Another Agent

If a conversation needs to be handed off — for example, when an agent is going offline or an escalation is required — transfer it by updating the assigned agent.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the conversation">
    Navigate to the conversation you want to transfer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Assigned Agent dropdown">
    In the right-hand sidebar, click the **Assigned Agent** dropdown.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the new agent">
    Choose the agent you are transferring the conversation to. The entire conversation history, including all messages, notes, and labels, remains intact and is immediately visible to the new assignee.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
The outgoing agent's messages and private notes remain part of the conversation thread, giving the new agent full context without any handover gaps.
</Note>
