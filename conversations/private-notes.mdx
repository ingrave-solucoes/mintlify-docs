Not every message in a conversation is meant for the customer. Private notes give you a dedicated space inside a conversation to communicate with your teammates — asking for advice, escalating an issue, or leaving context for the next agent who picks it up. Customers never see private notes, and the entire exchange lives alongside the public conversation thread so nothing gets lost in a separate chat tool.

## The Difference Between a Reply and a Private Note

Understanding when to use each mode is the foundation of effective collaboration in Chatwoot.

| | Reply | Private Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Visible to** | The customer and all agents | Agents and administrators only |
| **Displayed as** | A standard chat bubble | A distinct highlighted message with a lock icon |
| **Use for** | Answering the customer's question | Internal discussion, handover context, or escalation |
| **Triggers notifications** | Customer is notified | Only @mentioned agents are notified |

## Adding a Private Note

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the conversation">
    Click on the conversation where you want to leave an internal note.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch to Private Note">
    In the message editor at the bottom of the conversation, click the **Private Note** tab (next to the **Reply** tab). The editor background changes to signal you're in private-note mode.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write your note">
    Type your message in the editor. You can use the same formatting tools available in the Reply editor — rich text, attachments, and emoji.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit the note">
    Click **Add Note** to post it. The note appears in the conversation thread with a **lock icon**, clearly distinguishing it from customer-facing replies.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
Always confirm you are in the **Private Note** tab before sending internal comments. Accidentally sending a private discussion as a reply to the customer is one of the most common support workflow mistakes.
</Warning>

## @Mentioning a Colleague

When you need a specific teammate's attention on a conversation, @mention them directly inside a private note.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Switch to Private Note">
    Open the conversation and click the **Private Note** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type @ to trigger the mention">
    In the note editor, type `@`. A list of agents in your account appears.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the agent">
    Continue typing the agent's name to filter the list, then click their name or press **Enter** to mention them. The mention is highlighted in the note.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add context and submit">
    Write your message alongside the @mention to give the agent enough context, then click **Add Note**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
You can @mention more than one agent in a single private note. Each mentioned agent receives their own notification.
</Tip>

## How Mention Notifications Work

When you @mention an agent in a private note, Chatwoot immediately sends that agent a notification. The notification appears in the agent's **Notifications** panel (the bell icon in the top-right corner of the dashboard) and includes a link directly to the conversation and the note where they were mentioned.

The mentioned agent can click the notification to open the conversation, read the context, and respond — either with their own private note or by replying directly to the customer.

<Info>
Notifications are sent only to agents who are explicitly @mentioned. Simply posting a private note without an @mention does not notify any specific agent, though the note remains visible to all agents and administrators who open the conversation.
</Info>

## Viewing and Replying to Mentions

Agents can see all conversations where they have been mentioned from the **Mentions** view in the left sidebar. This makes it easy to track outstanding requests without scrolling through every open conversation.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Mentions view">
    In the left sidebar, click **Mentions** under the **Conversations** section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the flagged conversations">
    Each entry shows the conversation, the note content, and who mentioned you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Respond">
    Click on the conversation to open it. Add a private note to respond to your colleague, or send a reply to the customer if the issue is ready to be addressed.
  </Step>
</Steps>
