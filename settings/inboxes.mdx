An inbox is a connection between an external channel — your website widget, Facebook Page, WhatsApp number, email address, or any other supported platform — and your Chatwoot account. Once you have created an inbox, the settings page lets you fine-tune exactly how that inbox behaves: its name and avatar, the messages customers see when they start a conversation, and which automation features are active.

## Access inbox settings

Go to **Settings → Inboxes**. Every inbox you have configured is listed here. Click **Settings** next to any inbox to open its configuration page.

<Info>
  This page covers the settings available after an inbox has been created. To add a brand-new inbox and connect it to a channel (Website, WhatsApp, Email, etc.), use the **Add Inbox** button in the top-right corner and follow the channel-specific setup wizard.
</Info>

## Configuration tab

The **Configuration** tab controls the inbox's identity and the automated messages customers receive.

### Inbox name and avatar

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| **Channel Name** | The internal label shown in your sidebar and reports. Rename it to something your team recognizes instantly. |
| **Channel Avatar** | Upload a square image (PNG or JPG). This avatar appears in the chat widget and conversation header. |

Click **Update** after making changes.

### Greeting message

Enable the **Greeting Message** toggle to send customers an automatic first message as soon as they open a conversation. Type your greeting in the text field that appears. Use this to set expectations — for example, let customers know your response time.

### Away message

Enable the **Away Message** toggle to display a message outside your defined business hours. This reassures customers their message was received even when no agents are online.

### Email collect bot

When enabled, Chatwoot prompts customers to share their email address before the conversation begins. Collected addresses are saved to the contact record automatically.

## Features tab

The **Features** tab provides per-inbox toggles for several product capabilities.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="CSAT Survey" icon="star">
    Send an automatic Customer Satisfaction (CSAT) survey when a conversation is resolved. Results appear in your **Reports → CSAT** dashboard.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Auto Assignment" icon="shuffle">
    Automatically assign incoming conversations to available agents in this inbox using round-robin distribution. Disable it to rely on manual assignment.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Business Hours" icon="clock">
    Define the days and hours your team is available. Conversations received outside these hours trigger your away message instead of routing to agents.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Email Notifications" icon="envelope">
    Send an email to the customer when an agent replies, ensuring they never miss a response even if they close the browser tab.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

### Configure business hours

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enable the Business Hours toggle">
    On the Features tab, turn on **Business Hours**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your schedule">
    For each day of the week, toggle it on or off and set the opening and closing times using the time pickers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Update Business Hours** to apply your schedule.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Set your **Away Message** (on the Configuration tab) before enabling Business Hours so customers always receive a helpful response outside your operating window.
</Tip>

## Collaborators tab

The **Collaborators** tab controls which agents and teams can see and work conversations in this inbox.

### Assign agents

Search for agents by name and add them to the inbox. Only assigned agents will see this inbox's conversations in their left-hand navigation. Remove an agent at any time by clicking the **×** next to their name.

### Assign teams

Add one or more teams to the inbox. When a conversation arrives, it can be routed to any team assigned here. This is especially useful when multiple teams share a single channel (for example, a general support email address that routes to both a billing team and a technical team).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Search for a team">
    Type the team name in the Teams search field on the Collaborators tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the team">
    Select the team from the dropdown. It appears in the list immediately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save changes">
    Click **Update** to persist the assignment.
  </Step>
</Steps>
