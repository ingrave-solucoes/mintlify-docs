Customer Satisfaction Score (CSAT) is one of the most direct ways to measure how well your support team is serving customers. When an agent resolves a conversation in Chatwoot, the platform automatically sends a short survey asking the customer to rate their experience. The responses feed into a dedicated CSAT report, giving you an ongoing, data-driven view of customer sentiment.

## What Is CSAT?

CSAT measures how satisfied a customer is with a specific interaction — in this case, a support conversation. Chatwoot calculates your CSAT score using the following formula:

```
Satisfaction Score = (Total positive ratings / Total responses) × 100
```

A higher score means more customers left your conversations feeling good about the experience.

## How the Survey Is Delivered

Chatwoot sends the CSAT survey automatically when an agent marks a conversation as **Resolved**. The format of the survey depends on the channel the conversation took place in.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Website Widget">
    The CSAT survey appears as an interactive emoji-scale prompt directly inside the live-chat widget on your website. The customer taps an emoji to rate their experience and can optionally leave a text comment.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Other Channels (Email, SMS, WhatsApp, etc.)">
    For non-website channels, Chatwoot sends the survey as a message within the same conversation thread. The customer replies with their rating using the provided emoji scale and can add optional written feedback.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

The survey prompt asks the customer to rate their conversation on an emoji scale. After submitting a rating, the customer is invited — but not required — to leave additional written feedback.

## Enabling CSAT for an Inbox

CSAT is disabled by default on all inboxes. You need to enable it separately for each inbox where you want to collect ratings.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Inbox Settings">
    In the Chatwoot sidebar, go to **Settings → Inboxes**. Click the **Settings** link next to the inbox you want to configure.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the CSAT toggle">
    In the inbox settings page, locate the **CSAT** section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable CSAT">
    Toggle the CSAT switch to the **on** position.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your settings">
    Click **Update** to save the change. Chatwoot will now send a survey every time a conversation in this inbox is resolved.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Repeat this process for each inbox where you want CSAT surveys. Inboxes where CSAT remains disabled will not send surveys or appear in the CSAT report.
</Note>

## Viewing CSAT Results

Once surveys are being sent and customers are responding, you can analyse the results in the CSAT report.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the CSAT report">
    Click **Reports** in the Chatwoot sidebar, then select **CSAT**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the date range">
    Use the **Duration** dropdown to choose the time period you want to analyse. The default is the last 7 days.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter by agent (optional)">
    Use the **Agents** dropdown to focus on the survey responses handled by specific agents.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Report Metrics

The upper section of the CSAT report displays a summary of key metrics:

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Total Responses" icon="chart-bar">
    The number of customers who completed the survey in the selected period.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Satisfaction Score" icon="star">
    The percentage of positive ratings out of total responses. Higher is better.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Response Rate" icon="percent">
    The proportion of surveys sent that received a response, calculated as `(responses / surveys sent) × 100`.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

The report also shows an **emoji scale breakdown** — the percentage of customers who selected each emoji option — giving you a distribution of how strongly customers felt about their experience.

### Individual Responses

The lower section of the CSAT report lists every individual survey response, including the rating, any written feedback, the agent who handled the conversation, and a link to the conversation itself.

<Tip>
  Click any conversation ID in the CSAT report to jump directly to that conversation in your inbox, making it easy to follow up on negative ratings.
</Tip>
