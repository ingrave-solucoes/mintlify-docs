The Chatwoot Help Center lets you publish a self-service knowledge base where customers can browse articles and find answers on their own — reducing the volume of repetitive support requests your team receives. You organise your content into categories, write and publish articles using a rich-text editor, and serve everything from a URL that reflects your brand.

<Note>
  The Help Center is a beta feature that continues to receive improvements with each Chatwoot release.
</Note>

## Setting Up Your Help Center

Creating a Help Center involves three stages: creating a portal, organising it with categories, and writing articles. Work through them in order.

## Step 1 — Create a Portal

A portal is the top-level container for your Help Center. Each portal has its own URL and branding.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Help Center">
    Click the **Help Center** icon in the Chatwoot sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a new portal">
    Click the **New Portal** button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the basic settings">
    Complete the portal configuration form:

    | Field | Description |
    |---|---|
    | **Logo** | Upload your organisation's logo. This appears in the portal header. |
    | **Name** | An internal name used to refer to this portal within Chatwoot. |
    | **Slug** | Auto-generated from the portal name. This forms part of the public URL. |
    | **Custom Domain** *(optional)* | Enter a custom domain if you want to serve the portal at a specific URL (for example, `docs.yourcompany.com`). |

  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the portal">
    Click **Create portal basic settings**. Your portal is now ready, and you can start adding categories and articles.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  If you want the portal accessible at a custom subdomain (e.g., `help.yourcompany.com`), set up the appropriate DNS record pointing to Chatwoot before entering the custom domain here.
</Info>

## Step 2 — Create Categories

Categories structure your portal so customers can navigate by topic rather than scrolling through a single list of articles. Create categories before writing articles, as each article is assigned to one.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Categories panel">
    In the Help Center sidebar, click the **+** icon next to the **Categories** section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the category details">
    A modal will appear. Complete the fields:

    | Field | Description |
    |---|---|
    | **Name** | The category title displayed publicly on your portal. |
    | **Slug** | Auto-generated from the name. Used in the category URL. |
    | **Description** *(optional)* | A brief summary of what this category covers. |

  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the category">
    Click **Create category**. Repeat this step to create as many categories as you need.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Step 3 — Write and Publish Articles

Articles are the individual help documents your customers read. You write them using a rich-text editor directly inside Chatwoot.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a new article">
    Click the **New Article** button, which is available from almost every page within the Help Center section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write your content">
    Use the text editor to write your article. The editor supports headings, paragraphs, lists, links, code blocks, and other standard formatting options.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure the article metadata">
    Use the sidebar panel on the right to:
    - Assign the article to a **Category**.
    - Set the **Author** (defaults to you).
    - Update the **meta title** and **meta description** for SEO.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview your article">
    Click the **Preview** button to see how the article will look to customers before publishing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish the article">
    When you are happy with the content, click **Publish**. The article becomes immediately visible on your public portal.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Keep article titles short and descriptive — customers often scan titles when browsing a category. Start each title with the outcome or task, for example "Reset your password" or "Connect a WhatsApp inbox."
</Tip>

## Using Your Help Center URL

Once your portal is live, customers can access it at the URL Chatwoot generates from your slug. If you configured a custom domain, the portal is available there instead.

Share the Help Center URL in your:

- **Chat widget greeting message** — paste the URL in the welcome text so customers can self-serve before starting a conversation.
- **Email signatures** — link agents' email signatures to relevant Help Center articles.
- **Automated bot responses** — reference specific articles in automated replies.

You can also link directly to individual articles or categories by copying the URL from the preview page.
