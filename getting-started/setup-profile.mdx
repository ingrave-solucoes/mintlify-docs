Your agent profile controls how you appear to your teammates, how customers see you in the chat widget, and what email address Chatwoot uses to send you notifications. Taking a few minutes to complete your profile ensures a consistent, professional experience across every conversation you handle.

## Access Profile Settings

To open your profile settings, click the **kebab menu (⋮)** or your avatar icon in the bottom-left corner of the Chatwoot dashboard, then select **Profile Settings**.

<Info>
  Profile settings are personal to each agent. Changes you make here do not affect other agents in your account.
</Info>

## Update Your Profile Information

The top section of the Profile Settings page controls your public-facing identity — the details customers and teammates see.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Upload a Profile Image">
    Click the avatar placeholder and select a square image from your device. Chatwoot displays this image in the chat widget, conversation headers, and email notifications.

    <Tip>
      If you do not upload an image, Chatwoot automatically falls back to your [Gravatar](https://gravatar.com) if one is associated with your email address.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set Your Full Name and Display Name">
    Fill in the following fields:

    | Field        | Description                                                                                                    |
    | ------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
    | Full Name    | Your complete name, e.g. *Jane Doe*. Used in internal references and email communication.                      |
    | Display Name | A shorter name shown in the live-chat widget and outgoing emails, e.g. *Jane D.* or just *Jane*.               |
    | Email Address | The email address used for login and all Chatwoot notifications. Updating it logs you out immediately — sign back in with the new address. |
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save Your Changes">
    Click **Update Profile**. A success message confirms that your profile has been updated.

    <Warning>
      If you change your email address, you will be signed out automatically. Use the new email address and your existing password to log back in.
    </Warning>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Set Your Personal Message Signature

Your message signature is automatically appended to every email you send from an email inbox configured in Chatwoot. This is the equivalent of an email footer — typically your name, role, and contact details.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Locate the Signature Section">
    Scroll down on the Profile Settings page to the **Message Signature** section.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Write Your Signature">
    Use the rich text editor to compose your signature. You can include formatted text, links, and basic styling.

    ```text
    Jane Doe
    Senior Support Specialist | Acme Corp
    jane.doe@acmecorp.com | +1 (555) 000-1234
    ```
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save the Signature">
    Click **Save** to apply your signature. It takes effect immediately for all new email replies.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Configure Notification Preferences

Chatwoot can notify you about new conversations and messages through multiple channels. Customise these so you stay informed without being overwhelmed.

Navigate to the **Notifications** section of your Profile Settings page.

### Available Notification Types

| Event                                      | Description                                                                 |
| ------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| A new conversation is created              | Fires when any new conversation lands in an inbox you are assigned to.      |
| A conversation is assigned to you          | Fires when a conversation is specifically assigned to you.                  |
| A conversation is re-assigned              | Fires when a conversation you are working on is transferred to another agent. |
| A new message is created in a conversation | Fires when a customer replies to any conversation in your inboxes.          |
| You are @mentioned in a note              | Fires when a teammate tags you in a private note.                           |

### Notification Channels

For each event type, you can enable one or more delivery channels:

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Email Notifications" icon="envelope">
    Receive a summary email for each qualifying event. Useful when you are away from the dashboard.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Audio Notifications" icon="bell">
    Play a sound alert in your browser when a new message arrives — even if you are working in a different tab.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Push Notifications" icon="mobile">
    Receive native push notifications in the Chatwoot mobile app (Android and iOS).
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Check the boxes that match your preferred combination of events and channels, then click **Save Notification Settings**.

## Set Your Availability Status

Your availability status is displayed to your teammates and can affect how conversations are automatically routed to you.

In the bottom-left corner of the dashboard, click your avatar and select one of the following statuses:

| Status   | Meaning                                                                                               |
| -------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Online**  | You are active and available to receive new conversation assignments.                              |
| **Busy**    | You are logged in but occupied. New conversations may be routed to other available agents.         |
| **Offline** | You appear offline to the system. Automatic assignments are paused for your account.               |

<Tip>
  Set your status to **Busy** or **Offline** when you step away so the round-robin assignment engine routes conversations to teammates who are available.
</Tip>

## Change Your Password

<Steps>
  <Step title="Locate the Password Section">
    Scroll to the **Change Password** section on the Profile Settings page.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter Your Credentials">
    Fill in your **Current Password** followed by the **New Password** and **Confirm New Password** fields.

    Your new password must meet all of the following requirements:

    - At least one uppercase letter (A–Z)
    - At least one number (0–9)
    - At least one special character: `! @ # $ % ^ & * ( ) _ + - = [ ] { } | ' " / \ ^ . , < > : ; ? ~`
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save the New Password">
    Click **Update Password**. Use the new password the next time you log in.

    <Note>
      If you have forgotten your current password, log out and use the **Forgot Password** link on the login page to reset it via email.
    </Note>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Set Your Interface Language

Chatwoot supports multiple interface languages. To change the language displayed in your dashboard:

1. In Profile Settings, locate the **Language** dropdown.
2. Select your preferred language from the list.
3. Click **Update Profile** to apply the change.

<Info>
  The interface language you choose is personal to your account and does not affect the language your customers see in the live-chat widget.
</Info>
