Getting started with Chatwoot takes only a few minutes. Whether you are using Chatwoot Cloud or a self-hosted installation, this guide walks you through creating your account, verifying your email, setting up your first inbox, and bringing your team on board.

## Choose Your Setup Path

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Chatwoot Cloud">
    Use the managed cloud service at [chatwoot.com](https://www.chatwoot.com) — no infrastructure required. Follow the steps below to sign up.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Self-Hosted">
    If you are running your own Chatwoot installation, navigate to `{your_installation_url}/app/auth/signup` and follow the same registration steps. Refer to the [self-hosted documentation](/self-hosted/overview) for server setup details.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Create Your Account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Sign-Up Page">
    Go to [chatwoot.com](https://www.chatwoot.com) and click **Create a free account**. This takes you directly to the registration form.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Fill in Your Details">
    Complete every field in the registration form. All fields are required.

    | Field            | Description                                                                                                      |
    | ---------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
    | Full Name        | Your full name, e.g. *Jane Doe*                                                                                  |
    | Work Email       | A valid work email address, e.g. *jane.doe@company.com*                                                          |
    | Account Name     | Your company or organisation name, e.g. *Acme Corp*                                                             |
    | Password         | Must include at least one uppercase letter (A–Z), one number (0–9), and one special character (`!@#$%^&*` etc.) |
    | Confirm Password | Must match the password entered above                                                                            |

    <Tip>
      Use a work email address rather than a personal one. Chatwoot uses this address for login, notifications, and email-based conversation continuity.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Submit and Access the Dashboard">
    Click **Sign Up**. Chatwoot automatically logs you in and takes you to the main dashboard. A confirmation email with the subject **Confirmation Instructions** is sent to the address you provided.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Verify Your Email">
    Open the confirmation email and click the **Confirm my account** button. Email verification is required before you can use certain features such as sending outbound emails.

    <Info>
      If you do not see the email within a couple of minutes, check your spam or junk folder. You can also request a new confirmation link from the dashboard notification banner.
    </Info>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Set Up Your First Inbox

An inbox is a connected communication channel — for example, your website live-chat widget, a WhatsApp number, or a support email address. Create at least one inbox so your team can start receiving conversations.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Inbox Settings">
    In the left sidebar, click **Settings**, then select **Inboxes**. Click the **Add Inbox** button in the top-right corner.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select a Channel Type">
    Choose the channel you want to connect. Common starting points include:

    <CardGroup cols={3}>
      <Card title="Website Live Chat" icon="globe">
        Embed a chat widget on your website to talk with visitors in real time.
      </Card>
      <Card title="Email" icon="envelope">
        Connect a support email address and manage replies inside Chatwoot.
      </Card>
      <Card title="WhatsApp" icon="whatsapp">
        Link your WhatsApp Business number via WhatsApp Cloud API, Twilio, or 360Dialog.
      </Card>
    </CardGroup>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Configure the Inbox">
    Enter a name for the inbox (e.g. *Website Chat* or *Support Email*) and complete the channel-specific settings presented on screen. Each channel type has its own configuration fields — refer to the [Channels Overview](/channels/overview) for detailed instructions per channel.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add Agents to the Inbox">
    After saving the inbox settings, Chatwoot prompts you to assign agents. Select the agents who should have access to this inbox and click **Add Agents**. You can update this list at any time from **Settings → Inboxes → Settings** for the relevant inbox.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Finish Setup">
    Click **Finish**. Your inbox is now live and will start receiving conversations as soon as your channel is connected and active.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Invite Your Team

Add your support agents so they can start working on conversations alongside you.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Agent Settings">
    Navigate to **Settings → Agents** in the left sidebar.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Invite a New Agent">
    Click **Invite Agent**. Enter the agent's full name, email address, and choose their role.

    | Role          | Permissions                                                                                    |
    | ------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
    | Agent         | Can view and reply to conversations in assigned inboxes. Cannot access account-level settings. |
    | Administrator | Has full access to all inboxes, settings, reports, and user management.                        |
  </Step>

  <Step title="Send the Invitation">
    Click **Send Invite**. The agent receives an email with a link to set their password and access the dashboard.

    <Note>
      Agents only see conversations in the inboxes they have been assigned to. Make sure to add each agent to the relevant inboxes after they accept the invitation.
    </Note>
  </Step>
</Steps>
