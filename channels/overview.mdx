Chatwoot turns every messaging platform your customers already use into a single, unified support inbox. Whether your audience reaches you through a website widget, a WhatsApp message, an email, or a social media DM, your team responds from one place without ever switching tabs. Each connection is called an **inbox**, and you can create as many inboxes as you need — one per channel, one per brand, or any mix that fits your workflow.

## How Inboxes Work

Every channel you connect in Chatwoot becomes an **inbox**. Inboxes share the same agent workspace, so your team can handle conversations from multiple platforms in one queue. Each inbox has independent settings for:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Collaborators">
    Add or remove agents from an inbox and toggle automatic conversation assignment on or off. Only agents assigned to an inbox can see and respond to its conversations.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Business Hours">
    Set daily working windows and a timezone for each inbox. Chatwoot displays an offline message to customers who contact you outside those hours.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Greeting Messages">
    Send an automatic greeting when a new conversation starts. Customize the message per inbox so your tone matches each channel.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="CSAT Surveys">
    Automatically send a Customer Satisfaction survey when a conversation is resolved. Review results in the Reports section.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
